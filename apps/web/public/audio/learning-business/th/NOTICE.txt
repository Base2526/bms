BMS business learning guides - generated Thai narration

These MP3 files are synthetic narration of authored public guide text, generated
with FastThaiG2P, Thai Kokoro-82M and voice thai_som, the same voice used by the
Restaurant guide. They do not represent an endorsement by the model authors.
