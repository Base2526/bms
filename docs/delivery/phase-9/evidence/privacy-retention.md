# Privacy and retention packet

Owners: DPO and Legal. Inventory identity, device, location, proof, operational and audit data;
record purpose, lawful basis, visibility, processor, residency, retention clock, deletion behavior,
legal hold and data-subject request handling for each class.

Exercise export and deletion against synthetic records, including derived rows, object storage,
logs, backups and audit exceptions. Accepted evidence is an approved schedule and exercise record
showing complete, authorized handling without deleting records that law or audit policy requires.
