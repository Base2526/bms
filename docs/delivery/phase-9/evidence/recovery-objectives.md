# Recovery objectives packet

Owners: SRE and Operations. Approve RPO, RTO, backup frequency, offsite/region separation, encryption,
retention, restore authority, communication and reconciliation ownership. The local restore mechanics
in Phase 8 are preparation evidence only.

Perform a timed operator restore from an offsite backup into an isolated non-production environment.
Evidence must record backup age, start/end times, measured data loss, integrity/reconciliation results,
runbook deviations and owner sign-off proving the approved objectives were met.
